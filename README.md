## Project Overview


###SofTee Crochet Co. 

### Brief History 

SofTee Crochet Co. was founded in the year 2026 by Nqobile Nxumalo in Soweto, Gauteng, South Africa. The business started from a passion for creativity, craftsmanship, and handmade products. What began as a personal hobby has grown into an initiative that produces unique, high-quality crochet items. Each product is carefully handcrafted to ensure attention to detail and quality, with the aim of combining traditional crochet techniques with contemporary designs to provide customers with durable and personal products. 

### Mission  

The mission of SofTee Crochet Co. is to celebrate creativity through handmade Crochet. We strive to produce unique, quality products that support individuality and craftsmanship. Our purpose to bring handmade, meaningful items to people while growing a brand that values quality, community and sustainable making 

### Vision 

We envision SofTee Crochet Co. growing into trusted brand that is known for unique, handmade products. Our goal is to share the love of Crochet, support creativity and bring beautiful handmade items to more people across South Africa.

## Website Goals and Objectives 

### Specific Goals 

- **Goal 1:** Display products by showing clear images, descriptions, and pricing of all handmade crochet items. 
- **Goal 2:** Generate enquiries through contact forms, WhatsApp links, and order details to make it easier for customers to reach us. 
- **Goal 3:** Provide service information such as custom order options, delivery methods, and ordering steps. 
- **Goal 4:** Strengthen brand trust by sharing our company story, mission, and feedback from customer. 

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) 

- **KPI 1:** 20% increase in enquiries received through the website contact form and WhatsApp button. 
- **KPI 2:** 30% growth in product page views each month, tracked using website analytics. 
- **KPI 3:** 15% increase in website visits coming from Instagram and TikTok. 
- **KPI 4:** 10 custom orders completed directly through the website within the first 3 months. Young women and young adults aged 16 to 35 years. This group values fashion, self-expression, and unique accessories and they are looking for affordable, handmade, and personalized crochet items such as bags, hats, tops, and gifts that reflect their individual style.

## Part 3 Details
- Added 'script.js' , data and products.json
- ** Interactive elements: ** FAQ accordion (Enquiry/Voleenture), tabs(About Us), modal(Services), interactive map with Leaflet(Contact)
- **Advanced DOM:** 'createElement', 'appendChild', 'closest', 'dataset', 'IntersectionObserver', 'URLSearchParams'
- **Animations:** CSS keyframes and transitions, scroll reveal, count-up numbers
- **Dynamic content:** products loaded from JSON with 'fetch()' with search, category filter and sorting
- **Forms:** HTML5 validation attributes plus JavaScript error messages; the enquity form sneds with AJAX and shows a reply (cost and availability); the contact form builds an email for the visitor to send
- *SEO:** unique titles and descriptions, header order, image names and alt text, internal links, Open Graph tags, 'LocalBusiness' data, 'robots.txt', 'sitemap.xml'
- **Page speed and security:** compressed images, lazyloading, 'defer', '_headers' and '.htaccess' with a Content Security Policy, Subresource Integrity on LeafLet
